Terms of Service & Privacy Policy

1. Acceptance of Terms

By inviting Kani to your Discord server or utilizing its commands, you agree to these Terms of Service. If you do not agree with these terms, you must remove the bot from your server and cease using its automated features.

2. Description of Service

Kani is a Discord automation bot designed to monitor specified YouTube channels for scheduled and active livestreams, automatically generate Discord Scheduled Events, deliver customized role pings when streams go live, and provide a persistent personal reminder system for individual users.

3. Data Collection and Privacy

  • Server and Tracking Data: Kani stores the Discord Guild ID, configured Text Channel IDs, Role IDs, and YouTube Channel IDs to map API updates and send live stream notifications.
  • User Reminder Data: When setting a reminder, Kani stores your Discord User ID, channel ID, the reminder message, and the trigger timestamp.
  • Data Usage: All collected data is utilized strictly for Kani's stated automated functions. Kani does not sell data, share data with third parties, or use message data to train external models.

4. Acceptable Use

Users and administrators must use Kani in compliance with Discord's Terms of Service. You may not maliciously spam Kani's reminder system or use the reminder field to generate illegal or harmful content.

5. Data Retention and User Rights

Personal reminder data is automatically cleared immediately after the reminder has triggered. Administrators can permanently delete monitored YouTube channels and ping settings using the bot's removal commands. For manual deletion requests, contact the bot developer directly.
